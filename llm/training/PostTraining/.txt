  ### 3. Overfitting Prevention & Early Stopping Analysis
  #### Does the code have Early Stopping?
  No. Currently, Supervised_Fine_Tuning.py:400-475 runs in a fixed loop for max_steps (for step in range(start,
  args.max_steps)). It does not stop automatically if validation loss stops decreasing.

  #### Does it save the Best Checkpoint?
  No. It only saves checkpoints periodically (ckpt_interval=500) and at the final step (save(args.max_steps)). It does
  not automatically track min_val_loss or save a dedicated _best.pt file.
  #### What Overfitting Prevention is Present:
  1. Periodic Validation Evaluation: Evaluates val_ce and val_ppl on val.bin (500k tokens) every 200 steps (--
  eval_interval 200) and logs it to console and CSV so you can inspect convergence.
  2. Weight Decay: AdamW applies L₂ regularization (weight_decay=0.1) on all 2D+ tensors (linear projections and
  embeddings).
  3. Gradient Clipping: clip_grad_norm_ with threshold 1.0 to prevent sudden loss spikes from destabilizing weights.
  4. Cosine Learning Rate Decay with Warmup: Warmup for 100 steps, decaying smoothly down to 10% of peak LR
  (min_lr_ratio=0.1).
  5. MoE Load Balancing Loss: Keeps expert routing balanced to prevent expert collapse/over-specialization.
